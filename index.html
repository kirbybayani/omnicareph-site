Our Turnkey Solution

From empty space to operational center

OmniCare provides a complete turnkey solution for setting up hemodialysis centers, so clients can launch fully operational dialysis facilities efficiently and confidently.

1

Feasibility Study

Market and site assessment to validate demand, location, and financial viability before you commit.

2

DOH PTC and LTO Processing

Full regulatory compliance assistance, from Permit to Construct through License to Operate.

3

Architectural Planning

Facility layouts designed around DOH standards, patient flow, and efficient clinical operations.

4

Equipment Supply and Installation

Machines, water treatment, and consumables delivered, installed, and commissioned on site.

5

Staff Training

Hands-on training for clinical and technical teams to operate safely from day one.

6

Operational Support

Continuing supply, service, and advisory support once your center is up and running.

Flexible equipment lease options let you launch with lower upfront capital while OmniCare handles supply and maintenance.

Hemodialysis Solutions

Everything a dialysis center needs

From machines to consumables, water treatment to diagnostics, OmniCare is an FDA-licensed supplier supporting the complete ecosystem of a modern hemodialysis facility.

Flagship

NIKKISO DBB-EXA Hemodialysis System

Japanese-engineered, fully automated dialysis machines supplied, installed, and maintained by OmniCare. D-FAS automation handles priming, blood filling, and wash back, giving nurses more time for patient care, with flexible lease options available.

D-FAS Full Automation Online HDF / HD / HF / ISO-UF Integrated BPM and BVM RFID Patient Card 15" Touch Screen

Dialyzers

High-performance dialyzers engineered for effective, safe blood purification in every treatment session.

Bloodlines and Consumables

Complete lines of bloodline sets, needles, and dialysis consumables for day-to-day operations.

Water Treatment Systems

Reverse osmosis and water purification systems built to dialysis-grade water standards.

Diagnostics and Monitoring

ECG machines, glucose monitoring, and diagnostic devices for complete patient care.
